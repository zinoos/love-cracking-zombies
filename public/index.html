Rules

  • Field of view: you only see ahead (106°) and the cone turns with you. Right around you, you notice everything.
  • Weapon: pick one before every match — more damage or range costs speed.
  • Sword: 360° swing, 25 damage, no ammo, fastest movement. Invisible for 0.5 s after every kill.
  • Sergio: throws a record that turns around at the first wall and flies back. It hits both ways — you can only throw again once it is back in your hand.
  • Before the match: 8 s map vote, then two wheels each spin out a weapon that gets banned, then 5 s to pick freely.
  • Gold: paid out after every match — most for 1st place, still something for last. Spend it in the skin shop on colors with their own animation.
  • Mines: left click throws one up to 310 px over walls, a second left click detonates it — 99 damage in the blast. Enemies cannot see them.
  • Heavy weapons: double dash cooldown (4.8 s).
  • Stars: top half of the table gains, bottom half loses. Never below 0.
  • Grenades: 2 per match, 1 s fuse, 80 damage, blow up walls and bushes.
  • Bushes: enemies inside are invisible — unless they shoot or stand right in front of you. The crossbow stays silent.
  • Walls: block bullets and sight — until a bazooka or grenade blasts them to rubble.
  • Packs: green = health, yellow = full magazine + grenade.
  • Respawn: 3 s, then 1.6 s of invulnerability.
  • Maps: 17 of them, picked at random, square & symmetrical.
  • Modes: FFA (12 kills), 1v1 (8), 2v2 (15), 3v3 (20) — or until time runs out.
